# Stage 2 mission

Stage 2 is **The Sieve**.

It starts from Stage 1 mechanical survivors and decides which names deserve Stage 3 company/valuation work. Stage 2 is not a valuation model and not another broad market screen.

Mission questions:

- Is the IV spike explainable without an obvious binary trap?
- Is the name behaving like a meme/retail squeeze?
- Is the chart extended, broken, or near a healthier setup?
- Is current news supportive, neutral, or dangerous?
- Do analyst targets still leave upside?
- Is options liquidity suitable for limit-order execution and rolls?
- Is institutional ownership strong enough to avoid pure retail speculation?

Stage 2 outputs tier and eligibility; eliminated names do not advance to Stage 3.
